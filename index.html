Case 01

Interior Design Project SOP System

室内设计项目管理 SOP 系统

Problem: Complex design projects often lose clarity across stages, responsibilities, and follow-up actions.

Approach: Translate delivery experience into a stage-based SOP covering coordination, design development, consultant communication, site support, and review.

Output: A reusable project management SOP system for clearer execution and tracking.

将复杂项目流程拆解为阶段、责任、交付物和跟进动作，形成可执行、可追踪、可复用的项目管理 SOP。

SOP Project Delivery Risk Control
Case 02

AI Meeting Notes Workflow

AI 会议纪要与任务拆解流程

Problem: Meeting discussions are often bilingual, fragmented, and difficult to turn into reliable action.

Approach: Use AI-assisted prompts to organize discussions into notes, tasks, owners, deadlines, and risk reminders.

Output: A meeting-to-action workflow that improves post-meeting execution.

将中英文会议内容整理为会议纪要、待办事项、责任人、截止时间和风险提醒，提高会后执行效率。

AI Workflow Documentation Task Management
Case 03

Design Fee Calculator

设计费自动报价模型

Problem: Design fees can be difficult to compare when project scope and service levels are unclear.

Approach: Structure area, complexity, service content, and designer level into a transparent calculation model.

Output: A practical fee calculator that helps explain pricing logic and service scope.

通过面积、复杂度、服务内容和设计师等级，建立更清晰的设计费测算逻辑，帮助理解报价与服务范围。

Excel System Pricing Logic Service Scope
Case 04

Project Risk Checklist

项目风险预警清单

Problem: Project risks often appear late, after they have already affected time, cost, or delivery quality.

Approach: Build a checklist around design, drawings, pricing, construction, communication, and delivery milestones.

Output: A risk checklist that supports earlier review and clearer escalation.

围绕设计、图纸、报价、施工、沟通和交付节点建立风险清单，帮助项目提前识别问题并及时跟进。

Risk Control Checklist Delivery Management
Case 05

Hotel Design Company Database

酒店设计公司数据库研究

Problem: Hospitality design information is scattered across brands, projects, timelines, and design firms.

Approach: Map hotel brands, opening timelines, project types, and interior design companies into a structured research database.

Output: A research index for understanding hospitality design activity and industry positioning.

整理酒店品牌、开业时间、项目类型和室内设计公司，形成面向酒店设计行业的结构化研究资料。
